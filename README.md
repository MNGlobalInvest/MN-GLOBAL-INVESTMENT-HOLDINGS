# Executive Overview

MN Global Investment Holdings LLC is a privately held technology-focused holding company founded in 2013 and headquartered in Memphis, Tennessee.

The company operates as an integrated capital and technology platform focused on acquiring opportunities, developing operational systems, managing productive assets, and allocating capital toward long-term enterprise value creation.

MN Global combines:

- Business ownership
- Technology infrastructure
- Asset acquisition
- Artificial intelligence
- Automation
- Investment management

The company operates under a long-term ownership philosophy:

> Acquire intelligently. Operate efficiently. Allocate capital strategically. Compound enterprise value.
